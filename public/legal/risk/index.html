Version 0.1 · one page · last changed [date]

Trading forex, CFDs, synthetic indices and other leveraged products carries a high risk of losing money quickly. Most retail traders lose money.

  • Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Do not trade with money you need for rent, school fees, food or debts.
  • Training, accountability and MindSpace do not make losses less likely to happen to you. They help you follow your own plan; they do not make the plan profitable.
  • HexhaEdge never gives signals, never trades your account and promises no results, income or payouts.
  • Past results, including anyone’s screenshots, do not show what will happen next.
  • If trading is affecting your sleep, money or relationships, stop and talk to someone. MindSpace is there for that conversation.

In 2025, retail online forex traders in Kenya lost KSh 7.12 billion, according to Capital Markets Authority figures reported in July 2026.
